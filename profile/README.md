## 🤖 MISSION: BUILT TO COMPETE
Team Hyperion is a dedicated group of high school students from **Brisbane Boys' College** in Queensland, Australia. We specialize in the **Lightweight Soccer** division, combining advanced robotics, autonomous AI, and precision engineering to compete on the world stage.

### 🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS
- **2025 RoboCup Junior International (Salvador, Brazil):** - 1st Place — Lightweight SuperTeam World Champions
    - 14th Place — Individual Lightweight Soccer
- **2025 Australian National Competition:** - Undefeated National Champions
    - Reclaimed Australia's qualification spot for 2026

## 🚀 THE JOURNEY TO 2026
We are currently in active development for the **2026 RoboCup Junior International Competition in Incheon, South Korea**. Our focus is on pushing the boundaries of ball detection, strategic positioning, and hardware durability.
